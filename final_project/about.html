about this site

A soft recipe website that quietly asks how “healthy eating” shapes emotion.

Tiny Bites Club looks like a gentle and playful recipe site. It is filled with light meals, soft colors, and cute kitchen products. But underneath that sweet surface, the project asks a more serious question: how do food websites shape the way we feel about eating, our bodies, and ourselves?

My idea

I wanted to build a site that feels comforting at first. The visual style is soft, careful, and attractive. But the project also pays attention to the pressure hidden inside “healthy” food culture. Many food websites do not openly shame people, but they still shape desire, discipline, and guilt in quiet ways.

My design goal

I wanted the site to feel cute and inviting, while also making people slowly notice how design can affect emotion. The goal is not to judge healthy eating itself. The goal is to show how interfaces, product links, and food language can guide the way users see meals and even themselves.

What matters to me

I care about interactive storytelling, emotional design, and the small feelings that websites can create. I am interested in how digital spaces can look warm and gentle, while still carrying deeper questions about culture, behavior, and self-image.
